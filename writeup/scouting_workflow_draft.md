## Worked example: the proposed scouting conversation

Receiver A ran 4.54 seconds and B 4.52, yet their across-drill TURN profiles differ. The
pair was selected for an extreme movement contrast without NFL outcomes. Both profiles
use 13 drills; the displayed speed-out is one illustration. At the reference peaks,
B moves faster along a slightly wider local curve. Higher lateral acceleration therefore
does not identify the more efficient cutter.

The proposed film request is specific: **on comparable out routes, how does each receiver
approach the break, change direction and build speed afterward?** Record route depth,
alignment, motion, coverage, cushion and throw timing. Retain contrasting clips and missing
context. Those observations have not been assessed here.

The NFL evidence answers different questions. Development receivers show cross-drill
agreement, and turning carries modestly into game movement. Neither result establishes
either player's repeatability or separation advantage. Adding TURN to conventional
information did not establish lower athlete-mean separation prediction error in the
23-receiver evaluation. Supplied separation is measured at throw, rather than specifically
at an out cut.

The meeting produces a focused film question and an evidence gap. A TURN-based grade
change needs repeatable individual measurements and independently supported NFL performance
information beyond conventional evaluation. This study supplies no individual change
threshold or evidence that training TURN improves separation.
